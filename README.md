# Sundargarh Dream Home – Phase-wise Construction Plan

Interactive 3D concept and construction phasing reference for the owner's frozen final duplex design. **[Open the phase-wise construction plan](https://cloud-shelve-org.github.io/home-render/phases.html).**

## Frozen final design
- Approximate plot: 100 ft × 195 ft (verify by licensed survey).
- Ground floor: approximately 1,800 sq.ft.
- First floor: approximately 1,450 sq.ft.
- Total built-up target: approximately 3,250 sq.ft.
- Central courtyard: approximately 16 ft × 16 ft, open to sky.
- Double-height living room: approximately 22 ft × 20 ft footprint and 22 ft height.
- Puja room: NE, approximately 8 ft × 8 ft.
- Kitchen: SE, approximately 14 ft × 12 ft.
- Front open space: approximately 25 ft.
- Rear future extension: approximately 800 sq.ft.

## Revised construction sequence
1. **Phase 1 – Essential ground-floor home:** two bedrooms (approx. 14 ft × 15 ft each), two bathrooms (approx. 6 ft × 9 ft each), SE kitchen, NE puja room and a compact living room built in the final dining-room footprint. That compact room becomes the dining room later. The courtyard and future double-height living footprint remain unbuilt/reserved.
2. **Phase 2 – Main living room:** construct the final double-height living room in its exact master-plan footprint and connect through openings planned in the original drawings.
3. **Phase 3 – Complete ground floor:** complete courtyard edges/finishes, verandah, circulation and remaining ground-floor connections. Courtyard remains open to sky.
4. **Phase 4 – First floor:** bedroom 3, family lounge, gym, home theatre, gallery/indoor balcony and terrace.

## No-demolition requirements
- Final master floor plans must be completed before Phase 1.
- All future openings, lintels/frames, temporary closures and construction joints must be detailed at the outset.
- Reserve plumbing sleeves, capped branches, electrical conduits and service routes for later phases.
- Do not cut columns, beams or slabs in later phases.
- Foundations and RCC frame must be engineered for the complete duplex from day one.
- Do not cast a slab over the future courtyard or double-height living room.

## Site and boundary assumptions
- Preserve approx. 25 ft front open space, subject to survey and local rules.
- South road-facing boundary: approx. 100 ft, 6-ft fly-ash brick wall with RCC pillars and gates.
- Other three sides: approx. 490 ft total, 4-ft GI wire fencing.
- Keep the rear zone available for the planned approx. 800 sq.ft. extension.

## Important
This website is a visual concept and phasing aid, not a sanctioned or construction drawing. All dimensions, areas, Vastu directions, structure, statutory compliance and costs must be verified by qualified local professionals. The ₹15 lakh Phase 1 figure is a target only; obtain a measured BOQ and local quotations. Do not compromise structural safety, waterproofing or drainage to meet a budget.
